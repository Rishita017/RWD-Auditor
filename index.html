How it works

1 · Normalize
Adds https:// if missing.
2 · Fetch
Tries up to three CORS proxies in turn.
3 · Parse
DOMParser builds a virtual DOM.
4 · Analyze
Five rule-based analyzers run.
5 · Score
Weighted average: 25 / 15 / 20 / 20 / 20.

Each category starts at 100 and loses points per finding (a missing viewport tag costs 30, missing Open Graph tags cost 8), with a floor of 10. Checks are inspired by Nielsen Norman Group, Core Web Vitals, WCAG 2.2, W3C RWD guidance and Baymard research. Static HTML only: JavaScript-rendered content isn’t captured.
